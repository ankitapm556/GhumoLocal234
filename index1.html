Satkosia

About

The image of the Mahanadi flowing through a 22-kilometer-long gorge is breathtaking. Covering an area of 963.87 square kilometers, the Satkosia Tiger Reserve was formerly a part of the AbhayaAranya (Unending Forest range), which linked Odisha and Central India.The term Satkosia literally translates to "seven Kos," which is a measure of distance. often between 1 and 1.3 kilometers, depending on the area). Another name for it is the Gorge Sanctuary. The area is teeming with wildlife and is highly sought after by those who enjoy the outdoors.

Some of the most alluring tourist spots with overnight stays are found within Satkosia Tiger Reserve, each of which highlights a different aspect of this lovely retreat.

Beyond your wildest expectations, Tikarpada Nature Camp (TIPPS) on the banks of the breathtaking Gorge offers you an experience like no other.

Activites to do

Don't miss these must-do activities to make your stay at Satkosia Nature Camp truly unforgettable:

Birdwatching: Take in the breathtaking views of the unique and uncommon bird species that live in Satkosia. As you see these feathery marvels in their own environment, lose yourself in the tranquil beauty of nature.

Guided hikes: Experience a close-knit connection with nature by going on guided hikes across the woods. Discover Satkosia's hidden gems as you stroll along the picturesque trails and lush forests.

Boating Excursions: Savor leisurely boat rides that provide breathtaking views of the Gorge. Sail along the serene Mahanadi River while taking in the gorgeous scenery all around you.

Satkosia Nature Camp guarantees an enlightening and revitalizing experience with these activities, immersed in the world of nature.
